The Windows artwork (`vectorcraft-windows.svg` and its derived PNG and ICO files) is original
VectorCraft icon artwork drawn as SVG from the pastel Bézier-path concept generated with Arena's
image tool at the user's request and selected by the user on 2026-10-09. It is not a third-party
image file; the shipped Windows icon is the hand-authored SVG and its rendered derivatives.

Regenerate derived icons with `packaging/icons.sh`.
